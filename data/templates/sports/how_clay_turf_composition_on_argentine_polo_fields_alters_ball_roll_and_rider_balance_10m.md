First, breaking down the core elements of clay-loam soil structure and turf resilience.

Argentine groundskeepers engineer an optimal clay-loam blend—combining roughly twenty-five to thirty percent cohesive clay and silt with well-graded sand and dense, creeping Bermuda grass stolons.
