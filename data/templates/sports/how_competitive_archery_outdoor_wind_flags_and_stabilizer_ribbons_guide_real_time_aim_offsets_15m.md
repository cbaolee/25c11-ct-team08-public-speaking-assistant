Even passionate Olympic viewers fall into five common blind spots when watching outdoor archery wind reading.

Misstep one: assuming the bright triangular flags mounted above the targets are placed there merely for television decoration or national color. Misstep four: assuming every target lane on the exact same shooting line experiences identical wind. Stadium walls, camera towers, and trees routinely funnel wind gusts onto Lanes One through Four while leaving Lanes Five through Eight sheltered.
