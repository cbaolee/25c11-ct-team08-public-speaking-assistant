A traditional flat kayak paddle moves forward solely through hydrodynamic drag—pushing a wall of water backward so Newton's third law pushes the kayak forward, which inevitably wastes energy swirling water backward in turbulent eddies. A wing paddle is sculpted with a curved, cambered airfoil cross-section and twisted tip profile.
