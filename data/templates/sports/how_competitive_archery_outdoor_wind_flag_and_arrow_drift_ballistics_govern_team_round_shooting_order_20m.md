Even passionate Olympic viewers fall into five common blind spots when watching team archery matches.

Misstep one: assuming the three archers on a team must shoot in fixed alphabetical or bib-number order throughout the entire tournament. Coaches can rearrange the shooting order of positions one, two, and three between matches—and even adjust rotation rhythm dynamically during live ends.
