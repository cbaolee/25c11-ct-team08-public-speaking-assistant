The heart of the slice lies in its unique kinematic efficiency, where a compact swing path and early racket‑face rotation generate a low‑bouncing trajectory while conserving energy. {{ argument_slice_kinematic_core }}

When the ball comes in heavy pressure, the slice can be deployed mid‑rally to force the opponent to adjust timing and move forward, creating openings for the next shot. {{ argument_slice_in_rally }}

The downside is that over‑reliance on the slice may blunt offensive firepower and place extra strain on the wrist, especially on faster surfaces. {{ argument_slice_tradeoff }}
