Second, comparing approaches and philosophies across baton-passing styles.

Relay programs around the world divide primarily between two classic handover techniques: the upsweep pass and the downsweep pass. In the upsweep technique, the outgoing runner extends an open palm backward facing downward—forming an inverted V between thumb and fingers—while the incoming runner sweeps the baton upward into the palm, preserving natural upward running arm swing. In the downsweep technique, the outgoing runner extends a flat, upward-facing palm at hip height while the incoming runner chops the baton cleanly downward onto the open hand, offering a larger visual target for the incoming runner.
