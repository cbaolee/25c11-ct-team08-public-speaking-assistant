Stage two: how the damper lever controls airflow volume rather than direct weight.

Sliding the plastic damper lever on the side of the fan cage from one up to ten simply widens or narrows the intake louvers feeding air into the spinning fan blades. At setting one, nearly closed vents trap air inside the housing—letting the flywheel spin freely with minimal deceleration between strokes, which feels like a sleek, fast single scull gliding smoothly between catches.
