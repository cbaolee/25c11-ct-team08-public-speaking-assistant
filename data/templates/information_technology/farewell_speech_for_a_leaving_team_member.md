Okay, everyone, can I have your attention for a few minutes? Thank you.

As you all know, we're here today because {{ colleague_name }} is leaving us. {{ leaving_context }} And while we're happy for them, I think I speak for the whole team when I say: this one hurts a little.

{{ colleague_name }} joined us {{ time_at_company }} Back then, {{ team_situation_when_joined }}

I'd like to take a moment to talk about what {{ colleague_name }} has meant to this team. Not just the work, although there's plenty to say about that, but the kind of colleague they've been.

Let's start with the work. {{ key_contributions }} Some of the systems we rely on every day exist, and keep running reliably, because of the care they put into them. And I know that, for a long time to come, when someone opens a file and finds a clear comment explaining exactly why something was done that way, we'll know who wrote it.

But what I think we'll miss most is how {{ colleague_name }} worked with people. {{ personal_qualities }}

I also want to share a moment that, for me, says a lot about who they are. {{ memorable_story }}

[Optional: invite one or two teammates to add a short memory, about one minute in total.]

{{ colleague_name }}, on behalf of the whole team, thank you. Thank you for the code you've written and the problems you've solved, of course. But also thank you for the questions you asked that made us think, the reviews that made us better engineers, the help you gave without being asked, and the good mood you brought into the room, even on the days when production was on fire.

You're leaving behind a team that's stronger because you were part of it. And you're taking with you skills and experience that your new team is very lucky to get.

The tech world is small, and I'm sure our paths will cross again, maybe at a conference, maybe on an open-source project, maybe even as colleagues again one day. Please stay in touch. Our door, and our team chat, will always be open to you.

So, everyone, please raise your glasses, or your coffee cups, to {{ colleague_name }}. We wish you every success, and every happiness, in {{ next_chapter }}
