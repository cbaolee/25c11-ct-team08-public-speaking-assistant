Good {{ argument_time_of_day }}, everyone. Thank you for inviting us here today, and for the time your team spent with us over the past few weeks sharing your challenges and goals. My name is {{ argument_speaker_name }}, {{ argument_speaker_role }} at {{ argument_vendor_company_name }}, and with me today is {{ argument_colleagues_present }}.

Today, we'd like to present our proposal for {{ argument_project_name }}. I'll keep it focused. In the next ten minutes or so, I'll cover four things: what we understand about your situation, what we propose, how we'll deliver it, and what it will mean for your business. And then we'll leave plenty of time for your questions.

Let me start not with our solution, but with your situation, because a proposal is only as good as its understanding of the problem.

From our conversations with your team, we heard the following. {{ argument_client_current_situation }}

And this has real consequences. {{ argument_problem_impact_with_numbers }}

You also told us very clearly what success would look like for you: {{ argument_client_desired_outcome }}

Before I go further, I'd like to check: does that reflect your situation accurately? Is there anything you'd add?

Good. That understanding shaped our proposal from the start.

Now, our proposal.

In one sentence: {{ argument_solution_one_sentence }}

Let me explain what that means in practice, by walking you through a typical day after the solution is in place. {{ argument_solution_day_in_the_life }}

The solution has three main parts: {{ argument_solution_component_one }}; {{ argument_solution_component_two }}; and {{ argument_solution_component_three }}.

A few of our choices were deliberate, and they deserve a word of explanation.

First, we propose to deliver this in phases, rather than all at once. The first phase focuses on {{ argument_phase_one_focus }}, because that's where you'll see the biggest benefit fastest. This means you'll start getting real value within {{ argument_time_to_first_value }}, not after a year of waiting. And it means we can learn from real users and adjust before investing in the later phases.

Second, we've designed the solution to work with the systems you already have, specifically {{ argument_existing_systems }}, so your team doesn't have to change everything at once, and your existing investments are protected.

And third, we've focused on simplicity for the people who'll use it every day. The best system in the world fails if people don't want to use it. So we'll involve your users from the very beginning, with regular demos and feedback sessions.

How will we deliver it?

The project would run for approximately {{ argument_total_duration }}, in {{ argument_number_of_phases }} phases. {{ argument_timeline_overview }}

Throughout the project, you'll see working software every {{ argument_demo_frequency }}, and you'll have a clear view of progress through regular status reports. There will be no long silent periods where you wonder what's happening.

Our proposed team includes {{ argument_proposed_team }}. They'll be dedicated to your project.

You may be asking: why us?

{{ argument_relevant_experience }}

But more than our experience, I hope what you've seen during our conversations is how we work: we listen carefully, we ask difficult questions early, and we speak plainly about risks and trade-offs. That's the kind of partnership we'd like to build with you.

Speaking of risks, let me be transparent about the main ones we see, and how we plan to manage them. {{ argument_key_risks_and_mitigation }}

Now, let's talk about the investment.

The total investment for the project is {{ argument_total_investment }}. {{ argument_pricing_structure }}

But I'd encourage you to look at this not only as a cost, but in terms of the return. Based on the figures your team shared with us, {{ argument_expected_return }}. In other words, we expect the project to pay for itself within approximately {{ argument_payback_period }}.

And of course, these are estimates. We've been careful to keep them realistic rather than optimistic, and we're happy to walk through the calculations with your finance team in detail.

To draw this together.

You're facing {{ argument_problem_short_summary }}. We propose {{ argument_solution_short_summary }}, delivered in phases so you see value early, built to work with your existing systems, and designed around your users. With a dedicated, experienced team and a transparent process, we're confident we can deliver {{ argument_key_benefit_summary }}.

As for next steps, we suggest the following. {{ argument_proposed_next_steps }}

We'd be very proud to work with you on this, and we believe this project can make a real difference for your team and your customers.

Now, over to you: your questions, your concerns, and anything you'd like us to explain in more detail.
