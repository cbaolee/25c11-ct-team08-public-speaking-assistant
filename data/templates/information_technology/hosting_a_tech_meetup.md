[Opening, at the start of the event]

Good {{ time_of_day }}, everyone! Can everyone hear me okay at the back? Great.

Welcome to {{ meetup_name }}! My name is {{ host_name }}, and {{ host_role }} Thank you all for coming out tonight. I know it's {{ day_context }}, and you could be at home relaxing, so we really appreciate you choosing to spend the evening learning and talking tech with us.

Let's do a quick show of hands. Who's here for the very first time? Wow, welcome! And who's been to one of our meetups before? Good to see you again!

For those who are new, a quick explanation of what we're about. {{ meetup_introduction }} Our goal is simple: to share real experiences, the successes and also the failures, and to connect people who care about {{ meetup_topic_area }}

Before we start, a few quick housekeeping notes. {{ housekeeping_notes }}

And one important thing: we want this to be a welcoming space for everyone, whatever your level of experience, background, or opinions. So please be respectful, both in questions and in conversations afterwards. There are no stupid questions here. In fact, the "simple" questions are often the ones half the room was too shy to ask.

Tonight's program. {{ agenda_overview }}

Of course, none of this would be possible without our supporters. A big thank you to {{ sponsors_and_hosts }}

Alright, let's get started. Our first speaker is {{ speaker_1_intro }} Their talk is called "{{ talk_1_title }}." Please give them a warm welcome!

[Between talks]

Thank you so much, {{ speaker_1_name }}! That was fantastic. I especially liked {{ talk_1_highlight }} Let's give them another round of applause.

We have time for a couple of questions. Who'd like to go first? Please keep your question short, so we can fit in as many as possible.

[Questions, within the time planned in the agenda. If nobody asks at first, start with a prepared question:] I'll start with one of my own: {{ backup_question_1 }}

If you have more questions, {{ speaker_1_name }} will be around during the break, so please go and say hi.

Now, let's take a {{ break_length }} break. Grab some food and drinks, and take on a small challenge: talk to at least one person you didn't know before tonight. Ask them what they're working on. You never know where that conversation might lead.

[After the break]

Welcome back, everyone! I hope you enjoyed the food, and met some new people.

Our next speaker is {{ speaker_2_intro }} Their talk is "{{ talk_2_title }}." Please welcome them to the stage!

[After the second talk]

Thank you, {{ speaker_2_name }}! I think many of us will be trying {{ talk_2_highlight }} at work next week. Let's take a few questions.

[Questions, within the time planned in the agenda. Backup question:] Let me start with this one: {{ backup_question_2 }}

[Closing]

And that brings us to the end of tonight's talks. Let's give one final round of applause to both of our speakers.

Before you go, a few announcements. {{ upcoming_events }}

We're also always looking for speakers. If you've learned something interesting, solved a tricky problem, or even made an instructive mistake, we'd love to hear about it. You don't need to be an expert, and your first talk doesn't need to be perfect. Many of the best talks come from first-time speakers. {{ call_for_speakers }}

And we'd love your feedback on tonight. {{ feedback_method }} It really helps us make each meetup better.

Thank you all for coming, thank you again to our speakers, volunteers, and sponsors, and please feel free to stay a little longer to chat. See you next time!
