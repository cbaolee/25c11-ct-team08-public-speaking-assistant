Technology is moving faster than ever. Ideas that were research papers a few years ago are now in products used by millions of people. And with that speed comes a responsibility: not only to ask "Can we build it?", but also "Should we build it?", "Who will it serve?", and "How do we build it well?" Conferences like this one exist because no single company, university, or person can answer those questions alone. We answer them together, by sharing what worked, what didn't, and what we're still figuring out.

The person sitting next to you right now might become your future colleague, co-founder, or the one who helps you solve a problem you've been stuck on for months.
