Now, the most important part for you: how does this affect your work?

What you might notice: {{ visible_impact_for_colleagues }}

And what is not affected, so you don't need to worry about it: {{ what_is_not_affected }}

In four lines. The problem: {{ problem_short_name }}. What you'll notice: some disruption in the areas I mentioned, while everything else works as usual. What we're doing: a temporary fix soon, and a permanent fix after that. And what you can do: share the simple message with customers, and report anything unusual to us.
