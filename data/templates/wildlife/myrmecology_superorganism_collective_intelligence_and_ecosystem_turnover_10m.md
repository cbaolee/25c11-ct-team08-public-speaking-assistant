# The Unsung Janitors and Seed Planters

Beyond farming and architecture, {{ target_social_insect_group }} are the primary soil turners and sanitation crews of the terrestrial world. Across {{ study_forest_name }}, they move more earth per hectare than earthworms, aerating the ground and recycling {{ carrion_removal_percentage }} of dead animal matter within hours before it can rot.

And over {{ myrmecochorous_plant_count }} species of terrestrial plants rely on a process called **myrmecochory** -- producing seeds with a fatty snack pack attached called an elaiosome. Workers carry the seed safely underground into their nutrient-rich nest, eat the snack, and leave the intact seed planted safe from seed-eating rodents and wildfires.
