# Tracing Ghosts with Environmental DNA

Our forensic toolkit is also revolutionizing how we find and protect cryptic species before poachers reach them. Many endangered reptiles, like the {{ cryptic_target_species }}, are masters of camouflage -- spending {{ hidden_time_percentage }} of their lives buried in leaf litter or deep inside rocky crevices.
