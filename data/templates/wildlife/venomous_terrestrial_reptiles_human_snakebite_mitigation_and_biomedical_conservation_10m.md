# Introduction: The Most Feared Creature on Land

First, human-snake conflict is a massive, neglected public health crisis across the rural tropics. And second, {{ target_venomous_reptile_species }} are both irreplaceable agricultural guardians and **living pharmaceutical libraries** whose venoms have already saved millions of human lives.

> We kill tens of millions of venomous reptiles out of fear every year, destroying the very creatures that hold the molecular keys to modern medicine.
